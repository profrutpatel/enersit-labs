The Problem We Solve

Commercial AI Fails in Defense Environments

Modern defense and intelligence units manage petabytes of classified data — but existing AI solutions are fundamentally incompatible with sovereign security requirements.

Fragmented Data Silos

Terabytes of unindexed scans, OSINT signals, and archives sit across disconnected systems — making tactical search agonizingly slow and entity linking manual.

Cloud Data Leak Risks

Commercial LLMs (OpenAI, Claude) route data through foreign public cloud servers — strictly banned in military, intelligence, and national security environments.

AI Hallucination & Zero Audit

Generic LLMs fabricate facts without ground-truth verification. No audit trail, no citation matching — an unacceptable risk in mission-critical security workflows.
