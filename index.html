The Defense AI Problem

Commercial AI Is Incompatible With Defense Operations

Military units manage petabytes of classified data — but existing AI solutions fundamentally violate sovereign security requirements.

Vulnerability 01

Fragmented Data Silos

Terabytes of unindexed scans, OSINT signals, and field archives sit across disconnected systems — making tactical search agonizingly slow and entity linking entirely manual.

  • Hours lost manually linking threat entities
  • Slow cross-department tactical search
  • Delayed frontline critical decisions
Vulnerability 02

Cloud Data Leak Risks

Commercial LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini) route classified data through foreign public cloud servers — strictly banned under Indian Ministry of Defence air-gap policies.

  • Classified data routed to foreign servers
  • Banned across all defense & intelligence units
  • Zero local control over model parameters
Vulnerability 03

AI Hallucination & Zero Audit

Generic LLMs fabricate facts with no ground-truth verification and no audit trail — a catastrophic risk in mission-critical intelligence and security decision workflows.

  • Fabricated facts with no source citation
  • No tamper-evident audit trail
  • Cannot process classified document formats
